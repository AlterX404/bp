## Why it is worth paying for

1. The service catalog and detail views answer what the team can help with before a buyer calls. The chosen service comes into the form automatically.
2. The scope questions capture size, placement, location, quantity, installation and deadlines up front. Signage-only questions stay out of a fit-out enquiry, so buyers see fewer fields and sales gets a more relevant brief.
3. The review step and lead inbox give buyers a chance to catch errors and show sales a consistent brief to route or follow up on.

## Setup and maintenance

- Open dist/index.html directly on a desktop browser. The HTML is self-contained and works without a build, external fonts, remote images or a connection for the core journey. The phone and directions buttons open their respective device apps or Google Maps.
- For phone review, use node preview.mjs --lan from the extracted source folder on your computer. Open the printed phone URL with both devices on the same trusted Wi-Fi network. Stop with Ctrl+C. Windows may require allowing Node on the private network. The default node preview.mjs binds to 127.0.0.1 only.
- Maintain service descriptions, tags and estimate prompts in the services array. Maintain address, phone, hours and directions in the business object. Page copy and metadata are in the HTML. Replace the hero image and caption together with rights-cleared imagery.
- Leads are session-local and capped at 20. Requests use no external destinations and accept no uploads.
- For launch, have a developer connect a server endpoint to the approved email or CRM destination. Validate input again on the server, configure authentication for any staff inbox, set spam protection and rate limits, and report delivery only after the integration confirms receipt. Keep delivery credentials on the server. Confirm file size and type limits, private storage and scanning before enabling artwork uploads.
- Demo robots metadata and headers request no indexing. Keep the files offline, on trusted local Wi-Fi, or behind authenticated access for continued review.
